Welcome to Bourzik laboratory, an AI and data engineering hub for experimental projects. In my own time I build small, free web tools, mostly for writing systems and for learning languages. They run in the browser, store nothing, and work offline once you've opened them.

Tifinagh Converter

Write Amazigh in various languages' letters and read it back in Tifinagh, or the other way round.

Genus checker

Look up the gender of any German or other language noun, and drill the ones you keep getting wrong.

Bormap

Map ideas, people or data as a connected graph and follow the branches.
Built in the open on GitHub.
