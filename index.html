Bourzik ⴱⵓⵔⵣⵉⴽ

Bourzik laboratory is a workshop for small web tools, experiments in language, script, data and physics. Each one does a single thing, runs entirely in the browser, and keeps whatever you do on your own device. All free, all open.

Explore the tools
The tour was made entirely with AI: script, motion, images, voice, music and sound.

Tifinagh Converter

Write Amazigh in various languages' letters and read it back in Tifinagh, or the other way round.

Genus checker

Look up the gender of any German or other language noun, and drill the ones you keep getting wrong.

Bormap

Map ideas, people or data as a connected graph and follow the branches.

Fruit Jelly Knife

Pull, wobble and slice a translucent gummy fruit, a soft-body physics study rendered in WebGPU.

VECTORSPEC Engine Lab

Run four heat engines, a single piston, a turbo four, a turbofan and a ship's steam plant, and watch their cycles, limits and failures live.

CCDV-F Prep Hub

Study guide, flashcards and timed mock exams for the Claude Certified Developer – Foundations exam.
Built in the open on GitHub.
